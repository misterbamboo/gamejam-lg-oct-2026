Enjoyment = taste + color


0 < Volume <= 1
0 < dryness <= 1

Bounce and slimy and rough and soft and spiky > 0 

Reflection and refraction are element of [0,1]

Color =  tint * reflection + refraction * tint * (1 - reflection)

Reflection + Refraction = 1

Taste = 10 - abs(Heat - Request)

Tint is element of [0,1]

Surface = abs(rough - soft) + spiky

Dilution = volume/(volume + dryness)
Texture =  sqrt((bounce * slime * Surface)^(abs(reflection - refraction)))
Heat = 10 * abs(sin(Dilution) * cos(Texture) - tint)/(1 + sin(1))

Heat = 10 * abs((sin(volume/(volume + dryness)) * cos(sqrt((bounce * slimy * abs(Surface))^(abs(reflection - refraction)))) - tint))/(1 + sin(1))

Request is element of [0,10]


variables:
-Volume
-Dryness
-Bounce
-Slimy
-Rough
-Soft
-Spiky
-Reflection
-Tint

Cooking Pot Heat = AVR of all peppers' Heat
